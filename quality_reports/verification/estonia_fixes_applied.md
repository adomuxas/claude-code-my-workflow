## Rejected fixes

None of the report's proposed fixes was rejected after checking: every page I opened bore the report out. The following were applied differently from the report's wording, or left on purpose:

- **estonia-210**: the report proposed only adding `\autocite[239]{years_of_dependence}` for "Siberian gas". Misiunas & Taagepera p. 239 says the gas came "from the Urals", so I also changed "Siberian gas" to "natural gas ... by pipeline".
- **estonia-205**: the report proposed sourcing the clause "the Union as a whole ceased to converge on Western levels" separately, or dropping it. No corpus source was available, and the paper's own Table T5 contradicts it (USSR 3.4 against Western core 3.6 per cent a year in 1950--68, so there was no prior convergence). I dropped it.
- **estonia-224**: besides the page fix, I softened "faster than any other successor state" to "quickly". Korhonen p. 454 ranks Estonia against no other state.
- **estonia-27, -179** (Kasekamp 2017) and **estonia-180, -210** (olaf_stalin): pages not changed, per instructions (see for the authors). Only the wording was corrected where the corpus edition supports it (estonia-27 "outside Finland"; estonia-180 "mid-1950s").
- Optional page narrowings proposed for SUPPORTED instances were not applied: estonia-18, -20, -21, -28, -32, -121, -164, -165, -169, -188. estonia-232 (marking an elision in a direct quote) was applied because it is a quotation-accuracy fix.
- PARTIAL instances where the report proposed no change were left as they were: estonia-23, -56, -62, -139, -172, -181, -186, -207, -208, -219. Notes on several are below.

## For the authors

1. **1938 housing figure (estonia-88).** Valge's 1938 housing value-added is 32.0 m kr at current prices and 29 m kr at 1936 prices (Valge 2003, p. 2715, Table 42). The benchmark uses 29.0 alongside current-price figures for the other sectors (250.1 + 132.1 + 14.0 + 29.0 + 228.5 = 653.7, "654"). The text now says which price basis each figure is at, but the benchmark arithmetic and number macros are unchanged. Using 32.0 would raise GVA by 3.0 m kr.
2. **Norkus–Markevičiūtė 1938 figure (related to estonia-95).** main.tex:161 ("their 1938 figure, about 5 per cent below the new benchmark, is an independent check on it") and 04_limitations.tex:35 ("independent approximation, 5 per cent below the new benchmark") need a decision. "5 per cent below" matches Norkus's Valge-growth variant: 4,068 int$ 2011 = 2,552 GK$, against the benchmark's 2,685. That variant is not independent of Valge. Their own estimate, 3,796 int$ 2011 = 2,377 GK$ (Norkus 2023 p. 128), is about 11.5 per cent below. 02_method.tex:25 has been corrected; these two sentences have not.
