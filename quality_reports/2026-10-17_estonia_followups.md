# Estonia paper: follow-ups arising from the Latvia paper (2026-10-17)

## 1. Mention the 1962 agricultural crisis  [DONE 2026-10-17: §III sentence + §V paragraph, three bib entries added]
The Latvia paper (Section 4, 1950-68 paragraph) now explains the 1962 dip as the Baltic instance of the Union-wide
harvest failure and food crisis of 1962-3. The Estonia paper does not mention it, although:
- the Estonian crop index in `Datasheets/Sectoral volume indices Baltics 1919-1995.csv` falls from 134.2 (1961) to 109.1 (1962), -19 per cent, and recovers to 132.2 in 1963;
- Estonian GDP per head grew by only 0.6 per cent in 1962 (5,513 -> 5,547 Int$1990), against 3.7 per cent in 1961 and 5.3 per cent in 1963;
- Purre (1964, p. 55) records Estonia's grain deliveries to the all-Union fund falling from 30,000 t (1961) to 13,000 t (1962), potatoes 92,000 -> 66,000 t, vegetables 23,000 -> 16,000 t; fir needles as fodder in Jan. 1963 (pp. 34-5); the 1 June 1962 meat/butter price rise (pp. 53-4).

Sources (PDFs in `Papers/1962 crisis/`; bib entries already written in `Paper/latvia/bibliography_latvia.bib`, copy to the Estonia bib):
- `purre1964` Arnold Purre, *Soviet farming failure hits Estonia*, Problems of the Baltic 2 (Stockholm: Estonian Information Centre, 1964).
- `belkharoev2024` Kh. U. Belkharoev, 'Prodovol'stvennyi krizis v SSSR (1963 g.)', *Lobbirovanie v zakonodatel'stve* 3(4), 2024, 16-21 (USSR-wide: 1958 private-livestock ban, drought, bread rationing, 1963 grain imports).
- `andreenkov2021` S. N. Andreenkov, 'Sovkhozno-kolkhoznaia sistema v pervoi polovine 1960-kh godov', *Istoricheskii kur'er* 2021(4), 109-120 (1962-3 crisis, 31 May 1962 price decree, economists' memoranda).
