\section*{Appendix}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix}
\setcounter{table}{0}
\renewcommand{\thetable}{A\arabic{table}}

This appendix reports the annual series on which the paper rests. Table~\ref{tab:series} gives Latvian GDP per capita in 1990 and 2011 international dollars and the mid-year population for 1913 and every year from 1919 to 2024; Tables~\ref{tab:appsec1} and~\ref{tab:appsec2} give the constant-price volume indices of sectoral value-added for 1919--95 from which the GDP series of 1919--91 is built, with 1938 equal to 100; Table~\ref{tab:betadata} gives the initial income and growth of each European economy in the convergence regressions of Table~\ref{tab:beta}. The indices for 1992--5 are those of the Soviet-period series and are superseded, in the final series, by the modern series from 1991. The online appendix, available as an Excel workbook, contains the same series together with the 1935 and 1990 benchmarks by sector, the overlapping estimates at the two splicing points, the product-by-product composition of the industrial index, the comparator series used in the tables with their sources, and a source list for every series by year, keyed to the sections A.1--A.7 referred to in Table~\ref{tab:sources}. The series to 1944 refer to the borders of the republic and the series from 1945 to present-day borders.
